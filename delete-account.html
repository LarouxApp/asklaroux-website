Delete your account

You can delete your Laroux account and associated data at any time. Here's how, and exactly what happens to your data when you do.

Delete from within the app

If you have Laroux installed, this is the fastest way:

  1. Open the Laroux app.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Select Clear All Data & Sign Out.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account and data are removed right away.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or would rather not use it, email us and we'll process your deletion request:

support@asklaroux.app

Send the request from the email address associated with your account, or include the phone number on the account, so we can verify and locate it. We'll process verified requests within 30 days and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account and login credentials (email, phone number, and authentication records).
  • Your profile and Taste DNA preferences.
  • Your saved orders and dish history.
  • Your badge and streak progress.
  • Your share activity.

What is kept, and why

Menu scans and dish data captured during your sessions are retained in anonymized form. Once your account is deleted, this information is permanently disassociated from you — it is no longer linked to your identity, profile, email, or phone number, and cannot be traced back to you. We keep this anonymized menu data so that the community benefits of Laroux (shared menu coverage and recommendations) continue to work for other users.

If you have an active paid subscription, limited billing records may be retained where required for legal, tax, or accounting purposes, in line with our Privacy Policy. These records are not used to identify you within the app after deletion.

Summary
